Owl CalSync

by Systems Owl Inc.

Owl CalSync keeps your Microsoft 365 and Google Calendar in sync. Connect your own calendars, create one-way sync rules in either direction, and your events are copied between them automatically, so every calendar shows when you are busy.

  • Sync between Microsoft 365 and Google Calendar
  • One-way rules, in either direction, with full event details
  • You connect and manage only your own calendars

How Owl CalSync uses your Google data

When you connect Google Calendar, Owl CalSync asks permission to see your list of calendars (so you can choose one), and to read, create, update and delete events on the calendars you select in a sync rule. It uses this only to copy events between your Microsoft 365 and Google calendars as you configure, never sends invitations, never sells or shares your data, and never uses it for advertising or AI training. See our Privacy Policy for details. Owl CalSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
